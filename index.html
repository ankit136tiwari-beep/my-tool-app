Frequently asked questions

How is solar battery runtime calculated?

Multiply battery voltage by amp-hours to get total Watt-hours (Wh), then multiply by usable depth of discharge and inverter efficiency, and divide by your load in Watts.

What Depth of Discharge (DoD) should I enter?

Use 80%–90% for Lithium (LiFePO4) batteries and around 50% for Lead-Acid or Tubular batteries to protect battery life.
