How Toolbox calculators work

Each calculator has its own page and does one job. You enter your own numbers, such as balances, prices, fees, rates or wattage, and the tool applies a standard formula to show the result. Results depend entirely on your inputs and the assumptions stated on each page, so treat them as estimates for planning and comparison, not as quotes, tax advice or professional engineering advice. Fees, rates and tax rules change, so check current figures with the provider before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Toolbox calculators free?

Yes. You can use every calculator without paying and without creating an account.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. Open a calculator page in your browser, enter your numbers and read the result.

Is my data sent to a server?

The calculators are built to run in your browser, so the numbers you type are not uploaded for calculation. Read the Privacy Policy for details about the site, including any third-party services.

How accurate are the results?

Results follow the formulas shown on each calculator page, but they are only as accurate as the inputs and assumptions you provide. Real-world fees, interest rules and taxes may differ.

Can I rely on these calculators for financial or tax decisions?

Use them as a guide, not as professional advice. For major decisions, confirm figures with your lender, platform or a qualified adviser. See the Disclaimer.

Do the ecommerce calculators use official Amazon, Etsy or Shopify fees?

You enter the fee values yourself, because platforms change their fee schedules. Always check the platform's current fee page before relying on a result.

Do the calculators work on mobile?

Yes. The site is designed to work on phones, tablets and desktops, in light and dark themes.

How do I report an error or suggest a calculator?

Use the Contact Us page and include the calculator name and the numbers you used.
