    How Om Tools works & calculation methodology

    Who is Om Tools built for?

    Om Tools serves Amazon FBA and Etsy sellers, Shopify store owners, performance marketers managing Google Ads or Meta ROAS, freelancers pricing after-tax rates, borrowers comparing debt payoff plans, rental hosts, and solar installers seeking fast, transparent financial and technical estimates without spreadsheets.

    Which standard formulas power Om Tools calculators?

    Each calculator implements transparent, verifiable accounting, financial, and electrical engineering equations directly in your browser so you can audit every assumption:

    • Break-Even ROAS: 1 / Gross Profit Margin (accounting for COGS, shipping, and payment fees).
    • Rental Cash-on-Cash Return: (Annual Pre-Tax Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested) × 100.
    • Solar Battery Runtime: (Battery Wh × Depth of Discharge × Inverter Efficiency) / Load Watts.
    • True Loan APR: Amortized effective annual rate factoring upfront origination fees into net loan proceeds.

    Do these tools work across different countries and currencies?

    Yes. Because mathematical ratios, margins, and interest schedules are currency-neutral, users in the United States, India, the UK, Europe, Canada, and Australia can enter values in USD, INR, EUR, GBP, or any local currency while customizing local tax rates, carrier dimensional divisors, and marketplace commission fees.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are Om Tools calculators free and registration-free?

    Yes. Every calculator on Om Tools is 100% free to use with no paywalls, trial limits, account creation, or email sign-up required.

    Is my financial data sent to a server?

    No. All calculations execute locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. None of the numbers, fees, or personal scenarios you enter are uploaded to or stored on a server.

    Which currencies and regions do the calculators support?

    Om Tools uses currency-neutral financial and accounting formulas that work globally across USD, INR, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, and other currencies. You simply enter your local tax rates, shipping fees, or marketplace fee percentages.

    How accurate are the loan, ROAS, and tax estimates?

    Outputs strictly follow standard financial, e-commerce accounting, and engineering formulas displayed on each tool page. They serve as planning estimates and depend on the accuracy of the fees, rates, and assumptions you input.

    What is the difference between Break-Even ROAS and Target ROAS?

    Break-Even ROAS is the minimum Return on Ad Spend needed to cover product costs and fees with zero net profit (1 divided by gross profit margin). Target ROAS is higher because it factors in your desired net profit margin after ad spend.

    How is solar battery runtime calculated?

    Solar battery runtime equals usable watt-hours divided by total load watts, accounting for Depth of Discharge (typically 80%–90% for LiFePO4 lithium batteries vs. 50% for lead-acid) and inverter efficiency losses (usually 85%–92%).

    Can I use these calculators on mobile devices or offline?

    Yes. Om Tools is built with responsive mobile-first layouts, 44px touch targets, and light/dark themes. Once a calculator page is loaded in your browser, calculations run locally without needing server round-trips.
