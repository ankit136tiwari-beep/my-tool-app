Understanding Dimensional Weight in Modern Ecommerce Shipping

In ecommerce logistics, package freight charges are rarely determined solely by what a carton registers on a scale. Instead, major postal networks, air couriers, and freight forwarders evaluate packages through dimensional weight (also known as volumetric weight or cube weight).

Formulas & Calculation Examples

Metric Formula:
DIM Weight (kg) = [Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm)] / DIM Divisor (cm³/kg)
Imperial Formula:
DIM Weight (lb) = [Length (in) × Width (in) × Height (in)] / DIM Divisor (in³/lb)
Chargeable Weight:
Chargeable Weight = Max(Actual Weight, Dimensional Weight)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is dimensional weight in ecommerce shipping?

Dimensional weight is a pricing technique used by commercial carriers to bill packages based on cubic volume rather than scale weight.

What is a DIM divisor?

A DIM divisor is a numerical constant set by carriers representing cubic volume allowed per unit of weight (e.g., 5000 cm³/kg or 139 in³/lb).

Why is dimensional weight higher than actual weight?

When items have low physical density (like apparel, pillows, or small items in large boxes), dimensional weight exceeds actual scale weight.
