What this calculator does

The Google Ads Break-Even Conversion Rate Calculator shows the lowest conversion rate your campaign needs to cover its clicks, based on your real profit per sale. A break-even conversion rate tells you whether to scale, fix, or pause a campaign before you spend more. It also returns the break-even CPA, ROAS, maximum CPC and net profit from the same engine.

Features

  • Break-even conversion rate: CPC divided by contribution profit per conversion.
  • Break-even CPA calculator: the most you can pay per conversion without a loss.
  • Break-even ROAS calculator: includes non-advertising variable costs, not only product cost.
  • Conversion rate calculator: actual rate, gap to target, conversions needed.
  • Maximum CPC calculator: break-even and target-profit CPC.
  • Profit calculator: revenue, contribution profit, ad spend, net profit and margin.
  • Scenario comparison: current, improved and target projections, plus CSV export and print.

How to use

  1. Enter ad spend, clicks, conversions and average order value (required).
  2. Choose gross margin % or COGS as your profit basis, never both.
  3. Optional: switch to Advanced Mode for shipping, fees, refunds and targets.
  4. Press Calculate and read the status and result cards.
  5. Edit the scenario table to test a lower CPC or higher conversion rate.

Formulas and examples

  • Conversion rate % = conversions ÷ clicks × 100
  • Contribution profit = value − COGS (or value × margin) − shipping − other − (fees + refund %) × value
  • Break-even CPA = contribution profit
  • Break-even conversion rate % = CPC ÷ contribution profit × 100
  • Break-even ROAS = value ÷ contribution profit
  • Maximum CPC = contribution profit × conversion rate
  • Net profit = conversions × contribution profit − ad spend

Example: spend 1,000, 500 clicks (CPC 2.00), 25 conversions at value 100 with a 40% margin. Contribution profit is 40, break-even rate is 2 ÷ 40 = 5%, actual rate is 5%, break-even ROAS is 2.5x, and net profit is 25 × 40 − 1,000 = 0.

Practical use cases

Search campaigns, ecommerce, lead generation, small business PPC, agency and freelance reporting, performance teams, and monthly budget planning.

Limitations and assumptions

The tool does not connect to Google Ads, so you enter the data and results depend on its accuracy. Conversion tracking quality, attribution, delayed conversions, refunds and customer lifetime value change real profitability. It does not predict auction outcomes. Tax and fixed overhead are excluded unless you add them as variable costs. Scenarios are projections, not guarantees.

Frequently asked questions

What is a break-even conversion rate in Google Ads?

The conversion rate at which your ad spend equals the profit from the sales it generates.

How do I calculate the break-even conversion rate?

Divide your average CPC by the contribution profit per conversion, then multiply by 100.

What is a good break-even conversion rate?

Lower is better, because it is easier to beat. It depends on your margin and CPC, so compare it with your actual rate.

How do I calculate break-even ROAS?

Divide average conversion value by contribution profit per conversion. A 40% contribution margin gives 2.5x (250%).

Break-even CPA versus actual CPA?

Break-even CPA is the most you can afford per conversion. Actual CPA is spend divided by conversions. Actual below break-even means profit.

How do I find the maximum CPC I can afford?

Multiply contribution profit per conversion by your conversion rate. Subtract desired profit first to protect a margin.

Can I use it without connecting Google Ads?

Yes. You type in your numbers and everything is calculated locally in your browser.

Does a high conversion rate always mean profit?

No. If contribution profit per conversion is small or CPC is high, even a high rate can lose money.
