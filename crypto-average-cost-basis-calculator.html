How to Use the Crypto Average Cost Calculator

Managing cryptocurrency portfolios often involves executing dollar-cost averaging (DCA) strategies across multiple market prices. Simple mental math fails to reveal your true break-even point because fees and market levels vary across transactions.

  1. Select your target cryptocurrency: Choose Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), or input a custom asset ticker.
  2. Select your base display currency: Pick the single fiat denomination used across all transactions (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, or INR). Do not mix currencies within the same calculation.
  3. Enter each buy order: Specify the exact coin quantity purchased, the execution unit price, and any exchange or network gas fee paid out-of-pocket for that specific order.
  4. Review the instant breakdown: Observe the weighted average cost before fees alongside your fee-inclusive break-even price.
  5. Export or copy your records: Use the Copy Summary or Export CSV features to save your acquisition logs locally for bookkeeping.

How to Calculate Crypto Average Cost Basis

A volume-weighted average accurately scales each purchase according to how many coins were acquired at each respective price:

Purchase Value per Order (Vᵢ) = Quantity (qᵢ) × Unit Price (pᵢ) Total Quantity Acquired (Q) = Σ qᵢ Total Purchase Value Excl. Fees (V) = Σ (qᵢ × pᵢ) Total Out-of-Pocket Fees (F) = Σ fᵢ Total Acquisition Cost (C) = V + F Weighted Average Price (Excl. Fees) = V / Q Weighted Average Cost Basis (Incl. Fees) = C / Q

Average Crypto Cost Before and After Fees

Evaluating average cost solely from the exchange's execution price while ignoring trading commissions or gas fees underestimates your break-even threshold. This calculator presents both figures side-by-side so you can evaluate raw market timing (before fees) as well as net economic break-even (including fees).

Average Cost vs. Cost Basis vs. Break-Even Price

  • Weighted Average Buy Price: The volume-weighted mean of what you paid per coin on the open market, omitting trading friction.
  • Break-Even Acquisition Cost: The true out-of-pocket cash expended per coin, incorporating all platform commissions and gas charges.
  • Official Tax Cost Basis: The statutory basis used when reporting capital gains. In jurisdictions such as the United States, tax regulations generally require specific tax-lot methods (such as FIFO, LIFO, or Specific Identification) rather than a general portfolio average.
