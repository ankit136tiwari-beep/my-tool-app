What this calculator does

This free tool helps sellers calculate marketplace commission, custom percentages, additional fees, seller payout and estimated profit. All fee rates are entered manually by you. Nothing is fetched from any marketplace, and all calculations run in your browser.

How to use the Marketplace Commission Calculator

  1. Select a marketplace label or type a custom marketplace name.
  2. Choose your currency (no automatic conversion is done).
  3. Enter the selling price and order quantity.
  4. Enter your commission percentage.
  5. Choose the commission calculation basis.
  6. Enter fixed commission fees, if any.
  7. Switch to Advanced Fees to add payment, transaction, service, listing and custom fees.
  8. Enter product cost and your operating expenses.
  9. Review seller payout, total deductions and net profit.
  10. Use Compare Marketplaces, Reverse Commission or Target Selling Price if needed.
  11. Copy, print or download your results.

Calculation formulas and example

  • Percentage commission = commissionable amount × rate ÷ 100. Then the minimum is applied, then the maximum.
  • Fixed commission = fixed per order + (fixed per item × quantity).
  • Total commission = percentage commission + fixed commission.
  • Seller payout = net sales + shipping collected − marketplace deductions.
  • Net profit = seller payout − product costs − operating expenses (including advertising).
  • Profit margin = net profit ÷ net sales × 100. ROI = net profit ÷ product cost × 100.
  • Effective commission rate = total commission ÷ commissionable amount × 100.

Illustrative example (not an official fee schedule): selling price ₹2,000, commission 10%, fixed fee ₹20, payment fee ₹30, product cost ₹1,000, other expenses ₹100. Percentage commission = ₹200. Total deductions = 200 + 20 + 30 = ₹250. Seller payout = ₹1,750. Net profit = 1,750 − 1,000 − 100 = ₹650.

Use cases

Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Etsy and eBay sellers, Shopify store owners and independent ecommerce sellers can use it with their own fee values. It also helps new marketplace businesses, sellers comparing commission rates and sellers planning selling prices.

Important notes and limitations

  • Results are estimates. The tool has no preset fee rates. The marketplace names are only labels.
  • Tax and shipping treatment differ by platform and country. Choose the commissionable base yourself.
  • If tax is set to “included, exclude from base”, net sales and the commission base are divided by (1 + tax%).
  • Advertising is treated as your operating expense, not as a marketplace deduction, so it is never counted twice.
  • Per-order fees are charged once per order, not once per item.
  • Saved calculations stay only in this browser (LocalStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.

Check official fee pages (fees change by category, region, seller account and program): Amazon Selling Plans and Pricing, Amazon Seller Central Help, Flipkart Seller Hub, Etsy Seller Fees, eBay Selling Fees, Shopify Pricing.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Marketplace Commission Fee Calculator?

It is a tool that estimates the commission and fees a marketplace deducts from a sale and the amount you receive.

How do I calculate marketplace commission fees?

Multiply the commissionable amount by the commission percentage, then add any fixed fees.

How do I calculate a 10% marketplace commission?

Multiply the amount by 0.10. On ₹2,000, a 10% commission is ₹200.

How can I calculate seller earnings after commission?

Subtract commission and other marketplace fees from your sales revenue to get the seller payout.

What is the difference between marketplace commission and marketplace fees?

Commission is the main percentage charge on a sale. Marketplace fees also include payment, listing, service and other charges.

How do I calculate net profit after marketplace commission?

Subtract product cost, shipping, packaging, advertising and other expenses from your seller payout.

Can I enter custom commission percentages?

Yes. You enter every percentage and fixed fee yourself.

Can I compare commission rates across multiple marketplaces?

Yes. Compare mode lets you compare up to 5 marketplaces by total fees, payout and profit.

Does this calculator automatically retrieve marketplace fees?

No. It does not fetch any fee rates. Please check the marketplace's official fee page and enter the values.

Can I use this commission calculator for Amazon, Flipkart, Etsy and eBay?

Yes, if you enter the fee values that apply to your category, region and seller plan. It is not affiliated with any marketplace.
