How to Use This Debt Payoff Calculator

Getting out of debt requires a clear, mathematical plan. This debt repayment calculator with interest simulates your payoff journey month by month so you can directly compare debt snowball vs avalanche strategies using your actual account numbers.

Features of the Calculator

  • Dual Simulation Engine: Independently processes the debt snowball calculator and debt avalanche calculator models.
  • Automatic Payment Rollover: As debts are paid off, freed payments roll forward to the next priority target.
  • 100% Private: Calculations run locally in your web browser. No account required.

When to Use Snowball vs Avalanche

Debt Snowball

Best if you need quick psychological wins to stay motivated and clear smaller accounts first.

Debt Avalanche

Best if you want to minimize interest charges mathematically by knocking out the highest APR first.
