What this POD profit calculator does

This free tool helps print on demand sellers estimate profit per product and per order. It includes production cost, supplier shipping, customer shipping, marketplace and payment fees, advertising, discounts, returns and monthly overheads. Shipping matters because a low customer shipping charge against a high supplier shipping cost can turn a sale into a loss. All supplier and platform fees are entered by you.

How to use the print on demand calculator

  1. Select your POD provider and sales platform (labels only).
  2. Choose your currency.
  3. Enter the product's base and production cost.
  4. Enter the selling price, units per order and expected monthly orders.
  5. Add the supplier shipping cost.
  6. Enter the customer shipping charge, or tick free shipping.
  7. Add marketplace and payment processing fees.
  8. Add advertising, discounts and optional return costs (Advanced Mode).
  9. Review net profit, margin, markup and ROI.
  10. Use the pricing optimizer and the shipping scenario comparison.
  11. Copy, print or download your report.

Examples and calculation formula

Illustrative example (assumed numbers, not official supplier or marketplace fees): selling price $30.00, customer shipping $5.00, blank product $9.00, printing $4.00, supplier shipping $6.00, marketplace fee 6.5% of product plus shipping plus $0.20 transaction and $0.20 listing, payment fee 3% plus $0.30, advertising $3.00 per order.

  • Total revenue = 30.00 + 5.00 = $35.00
  • Production cost = 9.00 + 4.00 = $13.00; supplier shipping = $6.00 (shipping profit/loss = 5 − 6 = −$1.00)
  • Marketplace fees = 35 × 6.5% + 0.20 + 0.20 = $2.675; payment fees = 35 × 3% + 0.30 = $1.35
  • Total expenses = 13 + 6 + 2.675 + 1.35 + 3 = $26.025
  • Net profit = 35 − 26.025 = $8.975. Margin = 8.975 ÷ 35 = 25.64%. Markup = 8.975 ÷ 13 = 69.04%.

Formulas: net profit = total revenue − total expenses. Margin = net profit ÷ total revenue (including shipping revenue). Markup = net profit ÷ production cost. ROI = net profit ÷ (production cost + supplier shipping). Break-even price is the price where net profit is zero, found by a numerical search that includes percentage fees.

Important notes and limitations

  • Supplier costs, shipping rates, marketplace fees and taxes vary by provider, product, destination, country and plan. Nothing is fetched live and no fees are pre-filled.
  • Enter only costs that are not already included in your supplier's base product price. Use the checkboxes to avoid double counting.
  • Tax setting “price includes tax” removes the tax portion from product revenue. Shipping revenue is not adjusted. Tax rules differ by country.
  • Returns: the loss per return is refund + return shipping + replacement + handling + non-refundable fees − recoverable value. In expected mode it is multiplied by your return rate.
  • Income tax is only a simple optional estimate. Results are estimates, not financial or tax advice. Real profit depends on your actual transactions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Print on Demand Profit Calculator?

It estimates how much you keep from a POD sale after production, shipping, fees, ads and other costs.

How do I calculate print on demand profit?

Add product price and shipping revenue, then subtract production cost, supplier shipping, marketplace and payment fees, advertising and returns.

How do I include shipping costs in POD profit?

Add what the customer pays as revenue and what the supplier charges as an expense. The difference is your shipping profit or loss.

What is the difference between supplier shipping and customer shipping?

Supplier shipping is a cost you pay the POD provider. Customer shipping is revenue the buyer pays you at checkout.

How do I calculate Printify profit?

Enter your Printify product and shipping costs from your Printify account, plus your sales platform fees. The calculator does not fetch them.

How do I calculate Printful product profit?

Enter the Printful base price and shipping from your order or quote, then add platform, payment and ad costs.

How do I calculate print on demand profit margin?

Divide net profit by total revenue and multiply by 100. Markup divides net profit by production cost instead.

How do I find the break-even selling price for a POD product?

It is the price where net profit is zero. The calculator solves it including percentage fees and fixed costs.

Should I offer free shipping or charge customers separately?

Compare both in the scenario table. It shows profit using your numbers only and cannot predict how buyers will respond.

Does this calculator automatically fetch Printify, Printful, Etsy, or Shopify fees?

No. You enter every fee yourself, and the provider and platform choices are only labels.
